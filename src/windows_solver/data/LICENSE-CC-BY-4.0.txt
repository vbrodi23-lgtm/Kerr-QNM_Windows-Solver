Changes made for this package: all 63 relevant mode files were authenticated;
at the 392 exact spin overlaps, frequency columns in units of 2Mω were divided
by exactly two to obtain Mω and compared with the locally polished roots. The
packaged lattice stores the local determinant-polish values, not substituted
source-table values. Source member names, one-based row numbers, and absolute
comparison deltas are retained as attribution and validation evidence.
